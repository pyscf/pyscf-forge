This `csf_mem` branch represents my attempt to address GitHub issue #156. The goal is to prevent construction in memory of any array of size proportional to $e^{2norb}$ at the three hotspots discussed in that issue thread. In the `pspace` and `make_hdiag_csf` hotspots, the master branch currently builds Hamiltonian matrices in determinant subspaces and then applies the transformations implemented in csfstring.py (ultimately, `get_spin_evecs`) to put them into the CSF basis. The problem is that we run out of memory for this well before we run out of memory to store the CI vector. Although one could just separate the arrays into blocks dealt with sequentially to defeat this problem, I elected to attempt to implement the vector-coupling approach of Drake and Schlesinger (PRA volume 15 page 1990 year 1977, DOI:10.1103/PhysRevA.15.1990), which virtually all CSF work in the literature ultimately cites to. (This paper is available on my laptop in ~/Papers/PhysRevA.15.1990.pdf, but an LLM will probably have a hard time reading it, because it is a scan of a scientific paper from the 1970s with nonstandard diagrammatic equations everywhere.) One of my objectives in going this route was to build personal competency and intuition in how to do the quantum mechanics of angular momentum: just blocking over the arrays may have been easier, but it wouldn't have built skills that transfer to the different contexts of LASSI, spin-orbit coupling, etc.

I am currently stuck in debugging hell. The file I am trying to debug is `pyscf/lib/csf/csf_op.c`. The `pspace` and `make_hdiag_csf` target hotspots respectively are addressed by `FCICSFpspace_h0tril` and `FCICSFhdiag`, which in turn call down to diagonal and off-diagonal cases respectively of the functions `csf_Eai`, `csf_Sai`, `csf_EaiEaj`, and `csf_EaiEbj`. These four functions in turn call to different cases of `CGC_1e`, `CGC_2e_X`, and `CGC_2e_X_core`, which in my head refer to the diagrams in the Drake and Schlesinger paper: roughly, eqs (16), (26), and (27) respectively, although I think the latter two Drake equations are only special cases of a more general structure. The tests I am using to debug are in `pyscf/csf_fci/test`. `test_csf_2elec.py`, `test_csf_1eop.py`, and `debug_csf.py` are temporary files for debugging based off of `test_csf.py`. 

I believe that `csf_Eai` and `csf_Sai` are fully debugged, which is checked by the `test_csf_1eop.py` file. I also believe that the hdiag path is fully debugged for all four `csf_` functions, since the hdiag test in `test_csf.py` now passes without any restrictions or special case applied. But the more complicated `csf_EaiEaj` and `csf_EaiEbj` functions, which use the underlying `CGC` functions in more complicated ways, are not debugged in the `pspace` (off-diagonal) case.
